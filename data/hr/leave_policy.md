# Leave Policy — GoAified Technologies (Synthetic Sample)

Policy ID: HR-001 | Owner: Human Resources | Last reviewed: January 2026

## 1. Leave year
The leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December.

## 2. Types of leave and entitlement
- **Annual (earned) leave:** 18 working days per year, credited at 1.5 days per month.
- **Sick leave:** 10 days per year. A medical certificate is required for sick leave longer than 2 consecutive days.
- **Casual leave:** 6 days per year for short personal needs. Casual leave cannot be combined with annual leave.
- **Maternity leave:** 26 weeks of paid leave for the first two children, and 12 weeks for the third child onwards.
- **Paternity leave:** 10 working days, to be taken within 3 months of the child's birth or adoption.
- **Bereavement leave:** 5 working days on the death of an immediate family member.
- **Public holidays:** 12 company holidays per year, published by HR every December.

## 3. Carry forward and encashment
- Up to **10 days** of unused annual leave can be carried forward to the next year.
- Unused sick and casual leave lapse on 31 December and cannot be carried forward.
- Annual leave above the carry-forward limit can be encashed at basic salary when an employee leaves the company.

## 4. How to apply for leave
1. Log in to the HR portal (hrportal.goaified.example) and open **Leave > Apply**.
2. Choose the leave type, dates and a short reason.
3. Your reporting manager approves or rejects the request within 2 working days.
4. Annual leave of more than 5 days must be applied for at least **2 weeks in advance**.
5. Sick leave can be applied for on return to work.

## 5. Probation
Employees on probation (first 6 months) earn annual leave but can use at most 6 days of it during probation.

## 6. Contact
For questions about leave, email hr@goaified.example.
