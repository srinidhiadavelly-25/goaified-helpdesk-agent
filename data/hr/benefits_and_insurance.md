# Employee Benefits and Health Insurance (Synthetic Sample)

Policy ID: HR-002 | Owner: Human Resources

## 1. Group health insurance
- Every full-time employee is covered from the first day of employment.
- Sum insured: **INR 5,00,000 per year** for the employee, spouse and up to two children.
- Parents or parents-in-law can be added at the employee's own cost through the HR portal during the enrolment window (1–31 March).
- Cashless treatment is available at network hospitals. For non-network hospitals, submit claims within 30 days of discharge.

## 2. Term life and accident insurance
- Term life cover: 3 times annual fixed salary.
- Personal accident cover: INR 25,00,000.

## 3. Wellness
- Annual health check-up, free of cost, for employees above 30 years.
- Employee Assistance Programme (EAP): free and confidential counselling, 6 sessions per year. Call the EAP helpline listed on the HR portal.
- Gym and wellness reimbursement of up to INR 1,500 per month.

## 4. Learning and development
- Learning allowance of INR 30,000 per year for courses and certifications related to your role.
- Manager approval is required before enrolling. Submit the certificate and invoice for reimbursement.
