# Hybrid and Remote Work Policy (Synthetic Sample)

Policy ID: HR-004 | Owner: Human Resources

## 1. Hybrid model
- Employees work from the office at least **3 days per week** (Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday are team anchor days).
- The remaining days can be worked from home with manager approval.

## 2. Fully remote work
Fully remote work is allowed only with written approval from the department head and HR, normally for up to 3 months at a time.

## 3. Core hours
All employees must be available between **11:00 and 16:00 IST** on working days, regardless of location.

## 4. Requirements when working remotely
- Use only the company laptop and connect through the company VPN.
- Do not work from public Wi-Fi without the VPN.
- Keep confidential information out of sight in shared spaces.

## 5. Working from another country
Working from outside India requires prior approval from HR and Legal because of tax and data-protection rules. The maximum is 30 days per year.
