# Code of Conduct and Workplace Behaviour (Synthetic Sample)

Policy ID: HR-005 | Owner: Human Resources and Legal

## 1. Respect at work
GoAified Technologies does not tolerate harassment, discrimination or bullying of any kind.

## 2. Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH)
- The company has an Internal Committee (IC) under the POSH Act.
- Complaints can be raised in writing to ic@goaified.example within 3 months of the incident.
- All complaints are handled confidentially and retaliation is prohibited.

## 3. Conflicts of interest
Employees must declare any outside employment, business interest or relationship that may conflict with company interests, using the declaration form on the HR portal.

## 4. Gifts
Gifts from vendors or clients worth more than INR 2,000 must be declared to HR.

## 5. Whistleblower policy
Concerns about fraud or unethical behaviour can be reported anonymously at ethics@goaified.example. Whistleblowers are protected from retaliation.

## 6. Notice period
- Employees on probation: 30 days notice.
- Confirmed employees: 60 days notice. Unused annual leave cannot be adjusted against the notice period.
