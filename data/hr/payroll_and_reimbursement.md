# Payroll and Expense Reimbursement Policy (Synthetic Sample)

Policy ID: HR-003 | Owner: Finance and HR

## 1. Salary payment
- Salaries are credited on the **last working day of every month**.
- Payslips are available on the HR portal under **Payroll > Payslips** by the 1st of the next month.
- Form 16 is issued every year by 15 June.

## 2. Changing bank details
Employees must update bank details only through the HR portal (**Profile > Bank details**). For security reasons, HR and the helpdesk will **never** ask for bank account numbers, card numbers or Aadhaar numbers over chat or email.

## 3. Tax declarations
Investment declarations must be submitted on the HR portal by 30 April. Proofs must be submitted by 31 January.

## 4. Expense reimbursement
- Submit expense claims in the HR portal under **Expenses > New claim** within **30 days** of the expense.
- Attach original receipts for every item above INR 500.
- Claims are approved by the reporting manager and paid with the next salary cycle.
- Travel: economy class flights, hotel limit INR 6,000 per night in metro cities and INR 4,000 in other cities, daily meal allowance INR 1,200.
- Internet reimbursement for remote employees: up to INR 1,000 per month.
