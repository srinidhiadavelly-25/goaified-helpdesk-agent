# VPN and Remote Access Guide (Synthetic Sample)

Policy ID: IT-002 | Owner: IT Infrastructure

## 1. When to use the VPN
The VPN must be used whenever you work outside the office network to access internal systems such as the HR portal, code repositories, databases and shared drives.

## 2. How to connect to the VPN
1. Open the **GlobalProtect** app on your company laptop (it is pre-installed).
2. Enter the portal address: vpn.goaified.example
3. Sign in with your employee email and password, then approve the MFA prompt.
4. When the icon shows **Connected**, you can access internal systems.

## 3. Troubleshooting
- "Portal unreachable": check your internet connection and try again; restart the GlobalProtect app.
- Repeated MFA failures: re-register MFA at mfa.goaified.example.
- Slow connection: disconnect and connect to the nearest gateway (Hyderabad or Bengaluru).
- If the problem continues, raise a ticket in the IT Service Desk portal with a screenshot of the error.

## 4. Rules
- Do not install personal VPN software on company laptops.
- VPN sessions disconnect automatically after 12 hours.
- Personal devices cannot connect to the corporate VPN.
