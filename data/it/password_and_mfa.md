# Password and Multi-Factor Authentication Policy (Synthetic Sample)

Policy ID: IT-001 | Owner: IT Security

## 1. Password rules
- Minimum **12 characters**, with at least one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, one number and one special character.
- Passwords expire every **90 days**. The last 5 passwords cannot be reused.
- Never share your password with anyone, including IT staff. IT will never ask for your password.

## 2. Account lockout
After **5 failed login attempts** your account is locked for 15 minutes.

## 3. How to reset your password
1. Go to the self-service portal: password.goaified.example
2. Click **Forgot password** and enter your employee email.
3. Approve the request in the Microsoft Authenticator app (MFA).
4. Set a new password that follows the rules above.
If you cannot access MFA (for example, a lost phone), raise a ticket in the IT Service Desk portal (itsupport.goaified.example) or call the IT helpdesk at extension 4357. Identity is verified by video call before a reset.

## 4. Multi-factor authentication (MFA)
- MFA is mandatory for email, VPN, the HR portal and all cloud applications.
- Use the Microsoft Authenticator app. SMS codes are allowed only as a backup.
- If you change your phone, re-register MFA at mfa.goaified.example **before** wiping the old phone.
