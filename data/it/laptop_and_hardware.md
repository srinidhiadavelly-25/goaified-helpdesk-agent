# Laptop and Hardware Policy (Synthetic Sample)

Policy ID: IT-003 | Owner: IT Asset Management

## 1. Standard laptops
- Engineering and data roles: 32 GB RAM laptop (Dell Precision or MacBook Pro).
- All other roles: 16 GB RAM laptop (Dell Latitude or MacBook Air).
- Laptops are refreshed every **4 years**.

## 2. Accessories
Employees can request one external monitor, one keyboard-and-mouse set and one headset through the IT Service Desk portal.

## 3. Lost or stolen devices
Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone **within 2 hours** to the IT helpdesk (extension 4357) and to security@goaified.example so the device can be remotely locked and wiped. A police report (FIR) is required for stolen devices.

## 4. Damage and repairs
Raise a hardware ticket in the IT Service Desk portal. A loaner laptop is provided if the repair takes more than 2 working days.

## 5. Returning devices
All company devices must be returned to IT on or before the last working day.
