# Information Security and Incident Reporting (Synthetic Sample)

Policy ID: IT-005 | Owner: IT Security

## 1. What is a security incident?
Examples: a phishing email you clicked on, malware warnings, a lost device, sharing confidential data with the wrong person, or suspicious account activity.

## 2. How to report
- Click the **Report Phishing** button in Outlook for suspicious emails.
- For any other incident, email security@goaified.example **immediately** and call the IT helpdesk at extension 4357.
- Do not delete evidence, and do not try to investigate the incident yourself.

## 3. Data classification
- **Public:** can be shared externally.
- **Internal:** for employees only.
- **Confidential:** customer data, employee personal data, financial data — share only with people who need it.
- **Restricted:** passwords, encryption keys, health data — never share over email or chat.

## 4. Using AI tools
Do not paste confidential or restricted data (including customer or employee personal data) into public AI tools. Use only the company-approved AI assistant.

## 5. Security training
All employees must complete the annual security awareness training by 31 March.
